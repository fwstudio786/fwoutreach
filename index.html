How it works

  1. Drop a screenshot. The name, number and email are read for you and a pitch is written for that business.
  2. Check the message, edit it or tap a quick rewrite.
  3. Tap Open in WhatsApp or Gmail. The message is already filled in. You press send there.
  4. Mark it sent. It moves to Follow up after a few days if they haven't replied.
